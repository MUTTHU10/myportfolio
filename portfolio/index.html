Featured Projects

ROAD-SOS

RoadSOS is a smart road safety application designed to provide quick assistance during road accidents and emergencies. It allows users to register their personal details, vehicle numbers, and emergency contacts. With a single SOS button, users can share their location and send emergency alerts to their registered contacts through WhatsApp. The application also helps users identify nearby hospitals and report accidents using vehicle details, enabling faster emergency response and improving road safety.

Technologies: Python, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning

Safe-Her

SafeHer is a women's safety application designed to help women travel safely and get quick assistance during emergencies. It allows users to start a journey, share their live location, and confirm when they reach their destination safely. The application includes features such as a one-tap SOS button, voice-activated emergency alerts, and automatic notifications to trusted contacts when help is needed. SafeHer aims to enhance personal safety through smart technology, real-time location sharing, and rapid emergency communication.

Technologies: Data Analysis, Problem Solving
